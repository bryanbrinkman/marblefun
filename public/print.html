Print notes

  • Modular: each course cell exports as its own piece, numbered in track order — print them one at a time and assemble in sequence (the preview colors match the list below).
  • Scale: at 40 mm/cell the channel fits a standard 16 mm marble. Courses are TALL — check the printed-footprint figure before committing; 20 mm/cell makes a desk-sized display piece (with ~8 mm mini marbles).
  • Slicing: pieces are exported as overlapping closed shells — modern slicers (PrusaSlicer, Cura, Bambu) union these automatically. If your slicer complains, enable "union/merge overlapping volumes" or run the piece through its repair tool.
  • Orientation: files come Z-up with each module resting at Z=0. Most pieces print support-free; spirals and step-ramps may want tree supports.
  • The same seed always builds the same track — print the course your favorite race ran on. 🏁
