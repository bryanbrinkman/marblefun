Print notes

  • Bed fit: pieces are automatically segmented so every one fits the selected printer (256 mm on the X1 Carbon). Splits happen at natural block seams — every segment stays a closed solid. Anything that still can't fit is flagged red; drop the piece size a notch.
  • Modular: pieces are numbered in track order (01, 02, …) and segments stack bottom-up (a under b under c). Print, stack, glue, race.
  • Scale: at 40 mm/cell the channel fits a standard 16 mm marble. Check "assembled size" before committing — these courses are tall towers.
  • Slicing: pieces are overlapping closed shells — modern slicers (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura) union them automatically. If yours complains, enable merge-overlapping-volumes or run its repair tool.
  • Supports: anything that would print in mid-air (banner bars, arch tops, floating decorations) gets a slim 3 mm breakaway pillar baked in — gray in the preview. Print with slicer supports OFF, then snap the pillars off after printing. Untick "bake breakaway supports" if you'd rather use your slicer's own.
  • Orientation: files are Z-up with each piece resting at Z=0.
  • The same seed always builds the same track — print the course your favorite race ran on. 🏁
