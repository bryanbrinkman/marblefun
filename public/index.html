How Marble Run works

Races run automatically, around the clock. Every race is simulated from a published seed, so what you see is exactly what everyone else sees — and exactly what the results record.

    Pick a marble to cheer for it. Your pick is remembered on this device and stays your marble across tournaments — even after it's knocked out, it comes back in the next one. Following with the camera is separate: turn it on from the camera control whenever you like.
